## Key semantics / contradiction handling
1. Strict intrinsic 4D material-response versus cyberphysical functional 4D operation versus conventional static part printed by a capable machine are **separate observed output categories**.
2. A fully universal G1 **design target** and a safe incremental skeleton **currently installed capability** are distinct. Use CAN IF with measured domains; never skip safety-critical hardware to appear universal.
3. Three configurable bays are illustrative; autonomous printer CGX individuals are not the same as passive bays.
4. Cameras and structured-light can inspect visible surfaces, **not replace volumetric CT/NDE** for internal property proof.
5. Digital simulation supplants manual balancing paperwork, **not physical feedstock mass conservation, uncertainty or measured acceptance**.
6. Dynamic secondary process partitions can be manufactured/positioned within a still-qualified primary containment boundary; vacuum/purge/fault response require separate equipment.
7. New Neutrals **terrestrial product family** and `UTP-NEW-NEUTRALS-001` **functional interlayer taxonomy** are different namespaces, not contradictory names to silently merge.
8. A component atlas archetype is not a per-product independent hardware identity; exact manufacturer/lot instance and CGX child association remain versioned without duplicate canon.
9. Public-library/state university/industry lease deployment remains founder strategy, not verified contracts, pricing, insurance or trained/authorised personnel.
10. DBR archival history records must not become transferable title/currency or alter existing signed receipt meanings by convenience.
