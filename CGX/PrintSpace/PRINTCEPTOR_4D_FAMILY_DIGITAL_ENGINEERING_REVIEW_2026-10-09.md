## Interprintor process/module type grammar — inherit existing sources
**Roles, not additional arbitrary printer SKUs:** fixed depositor; robotic interchangeable printhead; roving printer; small child printer; bay-only passive staging zone; supply and feeder with Post-Mix carrier/concentrate logic; independent secondary atmosphere cell; scanner/metrology cell; functional electrical/optical test; subtractive/recovery or remanufacture cell; registered printer-to-printer/print-to-child dependency.

The UTP G1 programme's 17 functions remain the high-level comprehensive target: structure, conductor, dielectric, resistance/heating, capacitance, inductance/fields, bounded energy storage, emission, sensing, actuation, fluid routing, sealing, RF paths, thermal transfer, active logic, calibration/metrology, and CGX process/toolpath execution. Each installed printer advertises only demonstrated subsets.

The CGX geometry atlas's exact component instance is `archetype + dimensions + materials + ratings + interfaces + process route + calibration/evidence + revision`. Design-time geometry never invents measured data. Process composition, reactions and thermal/material survival are resolved through UTP/Raphael and Post-Mix owner science. 4D product state is `x, y, z, time + environment/material/state/evidence`. A genuine physical print over time requires measured constitutive/operational models; the included P0–P6 timeline is a symbolic workflow only.

## Source integrity and temporal semantics
- **Outer chamber:** one open working environment is default. Reusable panels/printed partitions create secondary temporary/permanent zones only under their separately measured isolation/protection windows.
- **Two arms:** start at two low mounted articulated positions but actual DOF/IK/collision/swept volumes/mount loads absent; the distance-only reach screen cannot be used for control.
- **Source family:** everyday, pro, lab, industrial, in-situ, orbital, and niche configurations should coexist as printer families/individuals, not force a uniform appearance or certified universal dock.
- **Printception:** PrintCeptor-0 safe manual bootstrap, self-printed noncritical tooling, first complete Butter Bot sealed-session article and functional test, partner evidence, then PrintCeptor A. Later child universal printer acceptance remains independent.
- **4D material criteria:** strict stimulus-responsive material state versus intelligently controlled integrated article are separately labelled, tested and DBR-evidenced. A static printed drawer is not automatically an intrinsically responsive 4D product merely because a 4D-capable station printed it.
- **Evidence:** rendered assets must carry `INTERACTION_PROXY / SYNTHETIC_SCREEN / NO_PHYSICAL_QUALIFICATION`, source IDs and revision/hashes. Nothing promoted to fabrication acceptance from a screenshot.
- **CGX/LightSpeed:** C0 deterministic controller is a separate hardware owner; C1 optional local reasoning, C2 optional network; no host or machine actuation in source review mode. Privacy/security/root promotion owner holds remain.
- **CCC/CYC:** library download/share remains owner/security/IP gated; these files are not automatically licensed/open commerce.
- **DBR:** archive recoverability default if network consolidation selected, awaiting actual owner-accepted service and restore tests.

## Next engineering sweeps (do not invent metrics)
1. Physical equipment/tool inventory → sourced parts/specifications and first safe seed BOM.
2. **Kinematic**: true dual-arm inverse kinematics, joint limits, tool reach/collision and seed-contact force/stiffness.
3. **Structural**: primary chamber window/rated supports, gravity load, deformation/resonance and separation between toolhead and material feed.
4. **Environmental**: process gas species, vacuum/pressure, leaks, valves, staggered secondary-zone isolation and personnel safety.
5. **Materials**: X→Y and Y→X, previous-layer cumulative damage, Post-Mix thermal/nonlinear chemistry, safe lot-qualified feedstocks and measurements.
6. **Temporal 4D response**: stimulus schedule, constitutive properties, delay/recovery/repeatability and appropriate calibration.
7. **Control/energy**: real power/current/heat sources, sensor uncertainty, no-AI C0 and optional C1/C2 execution behaviour.
8. **Post-build**: scaled Butter Bot closed-session operation, printed/sourced fractions, actual functional acceptance and iteration.
9. **Rollout**: Australian state library/university/industry pilots, separate procurement, legal IP, safety, insurance, and operational capacity approvals.
