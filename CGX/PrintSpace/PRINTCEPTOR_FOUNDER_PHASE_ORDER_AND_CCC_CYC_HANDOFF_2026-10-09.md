## Manufacturing build order — revised gates, same G06/G07 owners
**P0 — Machine/safety design:** Founder PrintCeptor-0 skeleton, two articulated robot bases and automatic tool exchange candidate, stable seed build paths, main chamber, isolable feed/service cavity, offline C0 local interlocks and C1 CGX context; no actuation without qualified equipment.
**P1 — First manually integrated bootstrap:** Assemble primary vendor-qualified components (e.g., motors, control circuits, containment hardware, sensors) and inspect the installed CGX device identity. Do not claim self-fabrication of purchased parts or fully rated 4D environment by appearance.
**P2 — Controlled self-made components:** Prioritise low-hazard printables (tool grips, claws, sample fixtures, floor port covers, service brackets, feed guides, drawers, and qualified internal partition prototypes). Define acceptance as dimensions, force/loads, alignment, electrical/chemical/environment compatibility where relevant and user-safety check. Requalify each install when function/hazard changes.
**P3 — Single-session Butter Bot as FIRST INTEGRATED PRINT ARTICLE:** Map sourced public CAD against exact measured geometry/BOM. Progress BB01 geometry/parts → BB02 tools/interfaces → BB03 integrated route **inside** the first Butter Bot manufacturing programme. Stage qualified purchased active components inside before sealing and use internal arms/child micro-printers to build/assemble with evidence of printed-vs-inserted content. Complete safe powered function test *before* outer enclosure opening; capture process and outcomes as signed DBR/CGX proofs. One-session = outer process seal never opened mid-run.
**P4 — Partnered test & iteration:** Use measured test packets, source rights, service/repair and qualification gaps to approach chosen state library/public demonstration, university technical reviewer and industrial/SQE operating partner. Outreach is not placement/lease acceptance.
**P5 — PrintCeptor A:** Design visibly refined and functionally expanded successor from proven PrintCeptor-0/Butter Bot/partner evidence. Recursive manufacture at a defined subset of functions can be a staged milestone; full UTP G1 child capable of the same 17-function basis has **separate stringent acceptance**, not automatically proven by making brackets or panels.
**P6 — Regional pilot then commercial expansion:** Initial Australian state-based reference network before general sales; exact states/territories included, institution selection, number of machines per partner, production volume, liability and funding require explicit proposals and independently signed agreements.

## Initial configurable chamber/floor
- **Default topology:** empty rectangular large work volume, transparent independently engineered outer enclosure. Attached tools and arms retract into protected volumes when unused; avoid fixed slots and wasteful internal supports.
- **Dynamic bays:** partition placement, geometry and seal profile compiled from job-specific physics. Frame/panel modules can be printed or reused after qualified material properties and interfaces. Secondary zones have separately supplied gas/pressure, monitoring, staged isolation and release; all internal zones remain within primary enclosure proof.
- **Floor matrix:** spaced protected multi-feed/utilities ports with configurable printed interface plates and mechanically rated registration points; plate identity is a CGX-referenced child, not independently controlling electronics unless equipped. Evaluate cable, electric, fluidic, gas, magnetic, thermal and tool-load routing without arbitrary live process interconnection.
- **Service cavity:** semi-controlled spool/feed zone may open/transition while outer process boundary stays sealed only through *independently verified* staged barriers/airlocks. Never repurpose as emergency noxious discharge without certified containment/neutralisation and operator/environment risk evaluation.
- **Two arms minimum:** seed-anchored and non-planar builds; printed grips/claws and tool-specific handoffs after metrology. Auto-bay micro-printers require own motion, energy, feed, environmental and identity budgets.
- **CGX inspection:** optically overlapping cameras and process sensors build a live surface/pose/process digital view; hidden volume requires separate NDE evidence, not camera-only CT claims.

## Butter Bot reference and reuse boundary
- [djnugent/ButterBot](https://github.com/djnugent/ButterBot) is a public *source-readable* repository with **CAD directory** and detailed build text: aluminium/acrylic/plastic chassis, tracked drive, servos, neck pulley, small controller, LED/audio and power. Historical actuator/electronics details are reference **not** modern printable qualified bill-of-materials. Source-readability does **not** imply a licence for commercial redistribution/modifications, especially given the fictional media character background.
- Different open-source software projects named Butter Bot are **not** evidence of a CAD or hardware licence on this specific repository.
- Founder intent: first New Neutrals robot reference print, then watches, phones and accessories; redesign the known Butter Bot geometry into a CGX-linked functional/solid-state variant using the printer's exact measured process/tool envelope.
