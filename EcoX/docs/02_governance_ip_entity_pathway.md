# Eco-X Governance, IP & Entity Pathway Dossier

## Purpose

This dossier defines the operating relationship between EMASSC, Römer Industries and Eco-X before final registration.

## Entity map

| Entity | Role |
|---|---|
| EMASSC | Eco-IP tank, origin architecture, custodial logic and long-horizon systems governance. |
| Römer Industries Pty Ltd | Current operating base, sponsor, commercial channel, InterSol coordinator and development platform. |
| Eco-X | Proposed ecological operator, restoration think tank, methodology holder, deployment arm and training pathway. |
| Independent Chair | Primary independent chair / secondary-founder-like establishment role. |

## Relationship logic

Eco-X relates to EMASSC in the ecological domain as Römer relates to EMASSC in the broader technical/commercial domain: Eco-X operates EMASSC eco-developed IP and documents derivative learning, field evidence and deployment method.

Römer may subcontract Eco-X for InterSol ecological envelope, restoration pilots, brown-waste loops, water-cycle reinforcement and methodology development.
