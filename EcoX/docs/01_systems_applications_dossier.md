# Eco-X Systems & Applications Dossier

## Purpose

This dossier maps Eco-X as a modular ecological system family: Embedded, Second Cycle, Brown Waste Upcycle and their stacked applications across local, national, international, in-situ and interplanetary timelines.

## Core engines

### Embedded

Embedded is the biological/substrate application family. It includes seed/spore/substrate modules, coir and brown-waste blocks, soil biology support, mycelial packets, pollinator habitat, edible/native plantings, erosion control and controlled habitat layers.

### Second Cycle

Second Cycle is the water-cycle reinforcement family. It supports capture, filtration, storage, reuse, controlled soaking, irrigation, humidity support, runoff reduction, cooling and closed-loop habitat water logic.

### Brown Waste Upcycle

Brown Waste Upcycle converts clean organic residues into ecological repair inputs: compost, mulch, biochar, coir blends, fibre, maintenance harvest and local substrate.

### Stacked systems

Eco-X designs site-specific stacks. One deployment may only need a garden block and passive water capture. Another may need substrate, irrigation, local training, food gardens, water storage, runoff capture and long-term MRV.

## Key real-world problem classes

### Murray-Darling style basin stress

Eco-X relevance: catchment reinforcement, riparian planting, soil-water retention, Second Cycle modelling, agricultural edge systems, biodiversity corridors and evidence-led basin-scale prototypes.

### Classic poverty and food-water insecurity

Eco-X relevance: minimum necessary infrastructure, local substrate production, training, edible ecology, water retention, community gardens and buy-a-block matched funding.

### Gaza / post-war reconstruction contexts

Public/semi-external phrasing should use “post-war / conflict-affected reconstruction contexts.” Eco-X relevance: low-infrastructure food-water stability, local training, substrate production, modular gardens and external conduct/safeguarding controls.

### InterSol

Eco-X relevance: active management site, ecological envelope, proof-of-theory environment, water-cycle reinforcement, brown-waste loops and collaborative R&D.

### Moon and Mars bases

Eco-X relevance: closed-loop water, substrate ecology, food-support systems, controlled biosphere logic, life-support agriculture and long-horizon interplanetary habitat support.

## System rule

Each deployment must answer:

- What exists now?
- What biome or function is desired?
- What is the minimum viable ecological infrastructure?
- What can the local community operate after handover?
- What can be measured?
- What evidence gate permits scale?
