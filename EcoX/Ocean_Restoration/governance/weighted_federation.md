# Weighted Federation Governance

## Principle

Participants contribute through role-specific operator or representative nodes. Decisions use transparent weighted
support as an advisory and coordination mechanism. A named accountable human or legal authority remains responsible
for final decisions where law, contracts, safety, fiduciary duty or ecological risk require it.

## Initial weight factors

- role weight
- project relevance
- verified contribution
- evidence quality
- conflict modifier
- availability modifier

`calculated_weight = product(all factors)`

Support is `+1`, abstention is `0`, and opposition is `-1`.

## Guardrails

- Any unresolved ecological or safety objection can place a mission into review or hold.
- Conflicts must be declared and can reduce or exclude voting weight.
- Decisions must retain identities, weights, rationale, timestamps, evidence and accountable authority.
- Cognigrex is the complete coordinated system, not an autonomous sovereign decision-maker.
