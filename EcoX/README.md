## Operating logic

Eco-X is the proposed ecological operations, restoration-development and deployment-governance arm for EMASSC-developed ecological systems. Römer Industries Pty Ltd is the current operating base, sponsor and commercial execution channel until Eco-X structure, leadership, registration and project pathways are confirmed.

## System families

- **Embedded:** substrate, seed, spore, soil, mycelial, habitat and biodiversity applications.
- **Second Cycle:** water capture, filtration, retention, reuse, soaking, hydrological support and closed-loop water logic.
- **Brown Waste Upcycle:** compost, mulch, coir, clean biomass, biochar and circular waste-to-repair systems.
- **Stacked / Lego-block ecology:** site-specific combinations of Embedded + Second Cycle + Brown Waste Upcycle.
